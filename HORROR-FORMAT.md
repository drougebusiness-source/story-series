# Horror format (the writer reads this file)

Used by any series whose `series.yaml` has `format: horror` (THE LAST DRAFT first). The model is @3amchoicess
("3 AM Choices | You Decide"): 62.4K followers from 5 posts, top part 5M views, one subway story told in 3 parts.

## What makes it work
- **The viewer is the hero.** Every line is second person and present tense: "You turn on your phone's flashlight."
  In THE LAST DRAFT the viewer is Cole: "you" teach these kids, "your" glasses are taped.
- **A narrator speaks every line.** The caption is the same words, shown word by word. Write for the ear: short spoken
  sentences, one idea each. Silence is a line on its own ("Silence.").
- **One place, one night, short arcs.** An arc is 2-4 parts (about 80 s each) in one location: the last car, the
  morgue corridor, the bridge at dusk. It ends on a twist, then a new arc starts somewhere new on the road north.
- **Plant the twist in part 1.** The subway girl's hand is "ice cold" and she "never got on at any station"; the pay-off
  in part 3 makes both clues click. Every twist must be fair: a viewer who rewatches part 1 should see it coming.
- **Every part ends on a timed dilemma.** A ticking line ("You have 10 seconds."), then the options read aloud:
  "Option A, you go into the tunnel with the man. Option B, you stay in the car with the girl. Comment A or B."
- **Open each later part on the choice.** "You chose to stay with the girl." Then the consequence, fast.
- **Dread over gore.** Knocking from outside. Handprints on the glass, small ones. A message from an unknown number.
  Show the reaction, the empty seat, the shadow; never the wound.

## Line rhythm (from the 5M-view part)
> Imagine you're riding in the last car of the subway. / It's midnight and there are only three people with you. /
> ... / The lights flicker and go out. / ... / Silence. / Then, someone knocks on the window, / from outside.

Long setup line, then shorter and shorter, then a one-word beat, then the scare. About 2.5-3 spoken words per second.

## Pictures
Flat, simple shapes are fine: this format wins on script and voice, not on detailed art. Dark frames with ONE light
source (flashlight, fire, a phone screen, a sunlit stairway). One clear subject per shot. Lots of close-ups: a hand on
a hatch, a face lit by a phone, a ladder going up into light. The viewer's character is seen only from behind, by
the hands, or in a reflection.

## In THE LAST DRAFT
- Keep the canon, the cast, the 300-mile road and SEASON1.md as the spine. Each SEASON1 beat becomes one arc
  (St. Agnes hospital = an arc in the dark morgue corridor; The Bridge = an arc at the bridge at dusk).
- The dead are the constant threat, but the best twists are about people: who is bitten, who is lying, who
  "never came in with us".
- Deaths and REWINDs work as before.
