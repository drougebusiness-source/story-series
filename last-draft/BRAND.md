## Main character: Cole Mercer
- **Age 34.** He was a high school chemistry teacher. Lanky, glasses mended with tape, a cardigan over a stab vest.
  He carries a chemistry kit, not a gun. He makes smoke bombs, water filters and medicine.
- **Believes:** every kid in his group gets to grow up. He promised their parents (most of them are dead).
- **Flaw:** he can't let anyone go, even when keeping them kills others.
- **Wants:** to get all 5 students to the **Harbor**, a rumored safe zone 300 miles north.
- **Needs:** to accept that he can't save everyone, and choose who.
- **Arcs:** the **Protector** (keeps the group whole at any cost) or the **Pragmatist** (hard calls and acceptable losses).
  The audience can turn him into a hero or into the kind of leader the kids fear.
