## Main character: Kael Torvin
- **Age 19.** A scrap diver on **Hollow**, a mining moon of the gas giant Veyra. Wiry, sun-scarred, goggles on his
  head, a breath-mask always half on. He dives into crashed ships for parts.
- **Has:** he can hear **the Current**, a hum in old machines that tells him where things are and what's about to break.
  It's getting louder.
- **Believes:** nothing. Every side lies, and you survive on your own.
- **Flaw:** he only cares about getting off Hollow. He'll sell anyone out to do it.
- **Wants:** a ship and a way off-world.
- **Needs:** something worth staying for.
- **Arcs:** **Unbound** (a rebel pilot and Current-wielder), **Concord** (an Attuned agent who hunts his own kind), or
  **Neither** (a free smuggler). Plus the running choice of training the Current or hiding it.
