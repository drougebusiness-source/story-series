## Look
- **Style:** painterly storybook fantasy (old book illustration meets a modern RPG). Warm parchment light, rich greens
  and golds, ink-map transitions between scenes.
- **Palette:** Parchment `#F1E3C3` · Forest `#2F5D3A` · Gold `#C9A227` · Wine `#7A1F3D` · Night Ink `#1C1A2E`.
- **Fonts:** headings in Cinzel Decorative; captions in Cormorant Garamond Bold.
- **Signature overlays:** **the d20.** Risky choices get a **true random d20 roll** (made by the pipeline before the
  episode is written, so the AI can't fudge it). A dice card shows it: "NEEDS 14+ ... 7 ... FAILURE".
- **Base image prompt:** *"Painterly storybook fantasy illustration, warm parchment light, rich forest green and gold,
  classic book illustration style, adventuring party, [SCENE], vertical 9:16"*

## Voice
- The narrator is a Dungeon Master: warm and teasing. "You *could* trust the raven. Many have. Few are still around."
- Crows' lies are in italics, with a 🐦‍⬛.
- The vote card: **"WHAT DOES WREN DO? A ... B ... C ..."** then, if it's a risky move, "ROLL: DC 14".
- Hashtags: #dnd #dungeonsanddragons #baldursgate3 #fantasy #interactivestory #youdecide #ttrpg #storytime

## Main character: Wren Ashby
- **Age 23.** Former squire of the Order of the Lantern. Short, freckled, cropped copper hair, armor that's too big
  (it was her father's). She fights well and talks badly.
- **Believes:** the Order is good, and someone inside it framed her for the theft of the **Lantern Relic**.
- **Flaw:** stubborn pride. She'd rather die than ask for help.
- **Wants:** to find the relic and clear her name.
- **Needs:** to find out whether the Order deserves her loyalty at all.
- **Companion:** **Crows**, a talking raven who lies about half the time. The audience slowly learns which things he
  says are true.
- **Arcs:** the **Paladin** (honor, redemption, retakes her place) or the **Oathbreaker** (learns the Order is rotten
  and burns it down). The dice can make either one hurt.
