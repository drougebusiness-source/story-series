# ROLL FOR FATE: Season 1 (10 quests)

🎲 = after the vote, a real d20 roll decides whether the move works. ☠ = can kill a character (plays, then REWINDS).

1. **Stripped.** Wren is stripped of her armor in the Lantern Hall. In the stable that night, a raven speaks.
   **VOTE:** A: follow the raven. B: break into the vault to see the "stolen" relic's case herself. 🎲 DC 13
2. **The Goblin in the Ditch.** On the road: an injured goblin with an Order seal in its pocket.
   **VOTE:** A: spare it. B: kill it. C: recruit it.
3. **Sister Maelle.** The Order's hunter catches up at an inn and offers Wren a deal: come back quietly and you'll get a trial.
   **VOTE:** A: go back for the trial. B: fight her. 🎲 DC 15 C: invite her to join the hunt.
   ☠ If B and the roll fails: *Wren falls, then REWIND.*
4. **The Bard's Curse.** Fennick's sung lies come true. The party needs to cross a bridge that doesn't exist.
   **VOTE:** A: have Fennick sing it into being. 🎲 DC 10 B: go around through the Barrow.
5. **The Barrow.** Osric can raise the dead to fight for them, which is forbidden magic.
   **VOTE:** A: let him. B: stop him.
6. **Brunhild's Forge.** Brunhild's secret: she forged the relic, and its "light" is a prison.
   **VOTE:** A: keep hunting it. B: change the quest to destroying it.
7. **Crows Talks.** Crows names the thief: the Order's Grand Lantern, Wren's mentor.
   **VOTE:** A: believe the raven. B: call it another lie.
8. **The Masquerade.** The party sneaks into the Grand Lantern's feast in disguise.
   **VOTE:** A: steal the relic quietly. 🎲 DC 16 B: expose him in front of everyone. 🎲 DC 12 (CHA)
   ☠ A failed roll means a party member dies covering the escape (the AI picks who from the votes so far).
9. **The Light Inside.** The relic opens. Something old speaks to Wren and offers her the Order.
   **VOTE:** A: refuse it. B: take the power.
10. **Season finale: Oath.** The Order is in flames or at her feet.
    **VOTE:** sets season 2: **A: Wren the Paladin, rebuilding the Order honestly. B: Wren the Oathbreaker, hunting
    every Lantern.**
