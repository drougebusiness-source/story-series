## The recipe that works (keep it for every episode)
- **Voices:** the owner picked every voice (26 characters, `<series>/cartoon/voices.yaml`, marked "OWNER PICK").
  Never change one without asking. ElevenLabs only.
- **Recording:** one fresh ElevenLabs take per phrase between `<break>` pauses (`voice._eleven_phrases`). Every take
  is checked by the local transcriber, a double listen and brightness, and retaken if needed.
- **No voice filtering, ever:** only a low-cut and volume. A hidden `clean()` filter once silenced every S and T.
- **Mix:** music and effects at most half the voice; the voice at about 80%, never slammed.
- **Pictures:** narrated shots are stills with slow camera moves. Only talking shots and entrances are AI-animated
  (lip-synced). Use `NO_NEW_VIDEO=1` for word or sound fixes.
- **Shape:** 25-40 s. Open by setting up the world. End with the question spoken over the vote screen
  ("What should X do? A, or B? Vote A or B in the comments."). Captions must match the speech exactly.
- **Scripts:** the owner approves every script before it's rendered (the writer is Sloane in Agent Office).
- Full rules: memory notes `story-voice-and-pacing`, `story-short-form`, `story-small-details`, `quality-rules-are-global`.
