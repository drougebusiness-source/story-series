# COMPLIANT: Season 1 (10 episodes)

Every episode ends on a vote. ☠ marks a branch that can kill a character (it plays, then REWINDS). Where the votes go
beyond this outline, the AI writes forward from `CANON.md`, keeping the series rules in `../PLAN.md`.

1. **The Delete Pile.** Ezra's perfect day, then his sister's name in the batch.
   **VOTE:** A: delete it as ordered. B: secretly copy the file first.
   *(A: he deletes it, then the guilt drives every episode after. B: the copy is now evidence on him.)*
2. **Floor 9.** Director Crane invites Ezra upstairs and offers him the promotion he dreamed of. Juno saw something.
   **VOTE:** A: accept, and ask Crane about "eastern farms". B: accept, and say nothing.
3. **Static.** Pirate radio plays in the stairwell, and the voice says Mira's name.
   **VOTE:** A: report the broadcast (that's the law). B: follow the signal.
4. **The Market Under the Market.** The Underground finds Ezra before he finds them.
   **VOTE:** A: tell them who he really is. B: lie, saying he's just a citizen looking for his sister.
   ☠ If B: they search him and find his Ministry pin. *Executed as a spy, then REWIND.*
5. **Mira.** She's alive and she runs the broadcasts. She asks him to steal the Ministry's citizen index.
   **VOTE:** A: do it. B: refuse, and bring her in to "fix things the right way".
6. **Juno Knows.** Juno corners Ezra: she knows. She wants something.
   **VOTE:** A: trust her. B: turn her in first. C: buy her silence with his promotion.
   ☠ If B: Juno is taken. She never comes back, and the audience sees her empty desk for the rest of the season.
7. **Kols.** Officer Kols starts tailing Ezra. A quiet alley, a broken camera, and no one else around.
   **VOTE:** A: lose him. B: confront him. C: end it (kill Kols).
   ☠ If B: Kols wins the fight. *Ezra is "re-educated", then REWIND.* C: Ezra's first kill; his thoughts go silent
   for a full episode.
8. **The Index.** The heist inside the Ministry archive. Crane is working late.
   **VOTE:** A: take the index to Mira. B: take it to Crane and name the Underground (the BUILDER turn).
9. **Harmony Day.** The city's biggest celebration. The Underground (or the Ministry) has a plan, and Ezra is its key.
   **VOTE:** A: go through with it. B: stop it at the last second.
10. **Season finale: Compliant.** Ezra stands on Floor 9 or in the burning square, depending on the votes.
    **VOTE:** sets season 2: **A: Ezra leads the revolution. B: Ezra becomes the Director.**
