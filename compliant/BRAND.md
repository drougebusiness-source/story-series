## Look
- **Style:** propaganda-poster art. Flat shapes, hard shadows, rough print texture, heavy diagonals (constructivist
  posters meet a modern graphic novel). The city is concrete and screens. Faces are half-lit.
- **Palette:** Ministry Red `#C8102E` · Ink Black `#111111` · Paper Cream `#EFE6D2` · Monitor Green `#39FF88`
  (screens only). Red is always the Ministry; green always means a secret or the Underground.
- **Fonts:** headings in Bebas Neue (all caps, wide spacing); captions in IBM Plex Mono, like a terminal readout.
- **Signature overlays:** a red "COMPLIANT ✓" stamp on Ministry victories; a glitching green
  "RECORD ALTERED" on Underground victories; a camera-eye icon in the corner of every frame (always watching).
- **Base image prompt:** *"Soviet constructivist propaganda poster style, flat graphic shapes, hard shadows, red black
  and cream palette, risograph grain, dystopian concrete megacity, surveillance screens, [SCENE], vertical 9:16"*

## Main character: Ezra Vale
- **Age 26.** Grade-3 Records Clerk, Ministry of Harmony, city of Meridian. Narrow, tidy, a neatly mended grey suit,
  a red Ministry pin he polishes. Black hair, cut to regulation.
- **Believes:** the system saved Meridian after the Collapse. Order means nobody starves. He's good at his job and
  proud of it.
- **Flaw:** he wants to be approved of. He'll talk himself into anything that keeps him "a good citizen".
- **Wants:** a promotion to Floor 9 and a flat with a window.
- **Needs:** to decide what he actually believes, not what keeps him safe.
- **Inciting moment:** a routine deletion batch has his younger sister **Mira Vale** in it, marked "UNPERSON". The
  family was told she moved to the eastern farms 2 years ago.
- **Two big arcs (the audience steers him):**
  - **REBEL path:** Ezra joins the Underground, becomes its best forger, and has to decide how far he'll go (sabotage →
    riots → a bomb).
  - **BUILDER path:** Ezra climbs the Ministry to fix it from inside, gets the power to save Mira, and slowly becomes the
    man who deletes people on purpose.
  - Either path can end with Ezra **running Meridian** or **dead**. Both are "saving the world" from where he stands.
